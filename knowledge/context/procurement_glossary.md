# Procurement Glossary (Team-Created, Synthetic)

- **MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity):** The smallest quantity a supplier will sell in one order.
- **Lead time:** Days between placing an order and receiving goods.
- **Reorder threshold:** The stock level that triggers a reorder.
- **Max stock:** The target stock level after a reorder.
- **Payment terms (Net 30, Net 45):** Payment due 30 or 45 days after delivery.
- **Cash on delivery (COD):** Payment made at the time goods are received.
- **Landing cost:** Purchase price plus delivery and handling fees.
- **Requisition:** An internal request to purchase; must be approved before becoming a Purchase Order.
