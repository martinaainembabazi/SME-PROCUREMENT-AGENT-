# Uganda SME Seasonal Demand Notes (Team-Created, Synthetic)

The following are illustrative patterns for SME building-supply shops in Uganda:

- **January–February:** Post-holiday slump; low construction activity.
- **March–May:** Rainy season; roofing (iron sheets), timber, and cement demand rises.
- **June–August:** Dry season; peak construction; highest cement and timber demand.
- **September–November:** Post-harvest; moderate construction; paint demand rises as households renovate.
- **December:** Festive season; short spike in paint, timber, and small hardware.

**Implication:** Reorder thresholds for seasonal items (e.g., paint, iron sheets) should be reviewed at the start of each season.
