# Supplier Selection Criteria (Team-Created, Synthetic)

When multiple suppliers offer the same item, compare on:

| Criterion | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Price per unit | 40% | Lower is better |
| Lead time | 25% | Shorter is better, especially for high-criticality items |
| MOQ flexibility | 15% | Lower MOQ preferred |
| Payment terms | 10% | Longer terms preferred (cash flow) |
| Reliability | 10% | Based on internal history (not tracked in this synthetic dataset) |

**Rule:** Do not select on price alone. A slightly higher price with much shorter lead time may be preferable for high-criticality items like cement and iron sheets.
